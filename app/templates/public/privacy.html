Your information deserves care

This notice explains how information is used to run the District Youth Bunotan gift exchange.

What we collect

We ask for your name, church, event registration, consent confirmations, and an optional gift preference. The system also creates a private registration reference and PIN. Only a secure Argon2 hash of the PIN is stored.

Why we use it

Information is used to review registrations, administer the district draw, support event distribution, and help protect the service from misuse. Gift preferences are optional and are not guarantees.

Who can see it

District administrators manage official records. Church leaders can view submission status only for their own church and cannot see assignments. A participant can reveal only their own recipient after verifying their reference and PIN.

Young participants

For a participant under 18, registration requires guardian consent. Please do not include sensitive personal details in the optional gift preference.

Your choices and questions

You may ask about access, correction, or other privacy concerns through your church youth leader or district contact. Ask the organizer how long event records are retained and how to request a deletion after the event.

This exchange notice does not replace the district's full privacy policy or explain retention periods not yet provided here. For contact details, see Contact.
