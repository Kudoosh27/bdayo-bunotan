Give thoughtfully

A gift is a kind gesture

A little care matters more than the price tag. Follow the specific instructions shared for your event.

The event budget

Stay within the announced range

Use the spending guidance shared by your district or church organizer. If you have not received it, ask before buying; do not assume an amount.

The recipient

Choose with care

Keep gifts age-appropriate, respectful, and suitable for a youth setting. A small practical or handmade gift can be just as meaningful.

Gift preferences

Use preferences as hints

A participant may share an optional preference during registration. It is guidance, not a promise or a guarantee that a specific item will be provided.

Questions

Check before you buy

Ask your church youth leader about event-specific restrictions, delivery arrangements, or accessibility needs.

Please follow the event's published instructions if they differ from these general guidelines.
