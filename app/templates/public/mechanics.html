The exchange

How Bunotan works

One district-wide draw connects approved youth participants across participating churches.

  1. 01

    Register once

    Choose the open event and your church. Keep your reference and private PIN when they appear after submission.

  2. 02

    Wait for verification

    Your registration is pending until district administrators review it. Church leaders can see only the status of submissions for their own church.

  3. 03

    The district draw is finalized

    After verification closes, administrators run one centralized draw for approved participants. If a withdrawal requires a redraw, the previous draw is superseded.

  4. 04

    Look up your own recipient

    When distribution begins, enter your reference and PIN on the lookup page. You will see only the recipient assigned to you.

Keep your PIN private

No church leader can look up assignments. Never give your PIN to someone asking you to reveal another participant's recipient.
