A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

When is my registration approved?

Submissions start as pending. A district administrator reviews them during the verification stage; your church leader can see your submission status but cannot approve it.

When can I see my recipient?

Your recipient is available after the district draw is finalized and distribution begins. Use your own registration reference and PIN on the lookup page.

I lost my PIN. Can I recover it?

The PIN is shown once and is not stored in readable form, so the site cannot display it again. Contact your church youth leader for help with next steps.

Can someone else see my recipient?

Public lookup reveals only the recipient for the participant whose reference and PIN were verified. Church leaders do not have access to assignments.

Will my gift preference be followed?

Preferences are optional suggestions and are not guarantees. Follow the event's gift budget and guidelines.

Why can't I register?

Registration is available only while an event is open. If the event or your church is missing, ask your church youth leader or use the contact page.
